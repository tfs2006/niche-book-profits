The idea isn't the problem. The niche is.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience — or writing talent?

No. The course assumes you're starting from zero, and low/medium-content books are planners, logbooks, journals, and activity books — structured pages, not prose. If you can use a web browser, you can follow along.

How much does it cost to publish on KDP?

Uploading to KDP is free; Amazon deducts the print cost from each sale before paying your royalty. Lesson 1 and the Royalty & Pricing Worksheet walk through the current royalty rates, print-cost math, and minimum prices — and show you where to verify the current figures in KDP's own help pages, since they can change.

How long until a book makes sales?

Honestly: it varies by niche and effort, and nobody can promise you a timeline. New titles typically take weeks to months to gain traction in Amazon search, and catalogs are built title by title. The course teaches that realistic, catalog-building approach.

Do I need paid research tools?

No. Lesson 2 teaches the research method using Amazon itself plus free sources, so you can validate niches before spending anything on tools.
